# Design System: Pitchcrew landing page

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Crew Board at Marketing Scale"**

The landing page is the desktop app's own Crew and Decisions vocabulary, enlarged for a visitor. The ground is one flat sheet of warm paper; nothing is drawn on it except type and hairlines. Work is held in trays pressed into the paper, and the only things with volume are the things you can press or pick up: agent cards, the installer rows, the active segment choice, and the glazed indigo button that is the one primary action of each surface. Round shaded objects are reserved for identity (pebbles) and state (beads). Everything else, including the packet document, the sheets around the browser panel and the chat reply, is a flat pane with a hairline edge.

The page tells one job's story with these materials and never with screenshots: four agent cards run the fictional Forma job in their grooves and stop on an ochre bead that is the visitor's to decide; the packet behind that decision opens as a claim tracer; a stage track carries the job across four stage objects. Type is Fraunces at weight 500 for display, over Source Sans 3 for everything that reads like the app. Light and dark are peers: the dark theme is the same clay lit by a faint rim, with the dye colours lifted so beads and chips keep their meaning.

The system reads calm and factual. It rejects the headline-over-screenshot template, rows of equal feature cards, terminal or hacker costume, HR-corporate gloss and gamified cuteness; its personality comes from the real objects of the product doing real work, labelled fictional.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Flat paper ground; clay volume only on pressables; recessed trays hold work; at most two depth levels on any surface.
- One glazed indigo primary action per surface; every other control is clay or ghost.
- The bead law carries state everywhere: plum executing, ochre waiting on you, primary completed, moss recorded positive, rose failed, an empty socket idle, and a raised ringed bead for the job's current stage.
- Identity pebbles (round, lit from the top left, tinted by role) are the only shaded circles besides beads.
- Fraunces 500 display with tight negative tracking over Source Sans 3 at 17px copy and a 14/13px meta scale.
- Transitions, never keyframes; transform and opacity only; the app's strong ease-out; a reduced-motion equivalent for every effect.
- Light and dark are peers from the same tokens; nothing is designed for light alone.

## Colors

A warm paper neutral family, five natural-dye role colours with soft and deep peers, and one glazed indigo primary; every value has a dark peer in the same family.

### Primary

- **Glazed Indigo** (`primary`): the one primary action per surface (Download, Approve packet, Approve), chat user bubbles through its soft peer, completed-stage beads and track fills, text links and the focus ring. Its deep peer is the hover state of link text; in dark it lifts to a pale periwinkle so it still reads as the actor on sumi paper.
- **Indigo Wash** (`primary-soft`): the user's chat bubble, the selected revised claim, and the primary chip. In dark it is a 16% tint of the lifted primary.

### Secondary

- **Teal, the Scout's tone** (`teal`, `teal-soft`, `teal-deep`): Scout pebbles, text selection, the selected quoted claim, the Reviewer's quote block and the "Quoted" check. `teal-deep` is the only tone used for the monospace source path and the posting flag line, so quoted evidence always reads teal.
- **Plum, executing** (`plum`, `plum-soft`, `plum-deep`): Writer pebbles, run bars inside grooves, the live bead and the ring around a working agent card, and the dashed outline on hidden posting instructions.
- **Moss, recorded positive** (`moss`, `moss-soft`, `moss-deep`): Reviewer pebbles, the approved outcome in the browser tray, the "Quoted" chip and board outcomes.
- **Ochre, waiting on you** (`ochre`, `ochre-soft`): the "Waiting on you" title and bead, the packet's approval state, the next-move line, the pending browser ask, and the "you" pebble. Ochre is never decorative; it only marks a decision that belongs to the visitor.
- **Rose, failed** (`rose`, `rose-soft`): the denied browser request. It appears nowhere else on the page.

### Neutral

- **Paper** (`paper`): the page and header ground, and the sticky stage track's fill. Nothing sits "on" paper except type, hairlines and trays.
- **Deep Paper** (`paper-deep`): the recessed fill of trays, grooves, beads at rest, chat threads, the segment track and default chips.
- **Surface / Bright Surface** (`surface`, `surface-bright`): the clay fill of pressables (surface, lit to bright at the top left) and the flat panes (documents, sheets, chat replies, menus) at bright.
- **Ink / Soft Ink / Muted / Faint** (`ink`, `ink-soft`, `muted`, `faint`): headings and object titles; section copy, ledes and object text; meta, fine print and groove labels; disabled or empty.
- **Hairline** (`hairline`): an 11% ink tint for every border, divider and pane edge; the only line the system draws.

### Named Rules

**The Bead Law.** A bead's tone is its meaning and nothing else: plum means an agent is executing, ochre means the decision is the visitor's, primary means a step completed, moss means a recorded positive outcome, rose means failed, and an empty socket means idle. A job's current stage at rest is a neutral raised bead ringed in primary. No bead tone may be chosen for looks.

**The One Glaze Rule.** Each surface (header, waiting tray, packet footer, browser tray, download sheet) carries exactly one glazed indigo button. A second action on that surface is clay; a third is ghost.

**The Evidence Is Teal Rule.** Profile quotations, their source paths and the posting's "read as data" flag use teal and `teal-deep`; nothing else on the page is teal except the Scout's pebble.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Fraunces Variable (with Georgia, serif)
**Body Font:** Source Sans 3 (with sans-serif)
**Label/Mono Font:** the system monospace stack, for source paths only

**Character:** Fraunces at 500 with negative tracking down to -0.035em gives the headlines a warm, slightly old-fashioned confidence; Source Sans 3 carries everything that is an object from the app, so cards, trays and beads read exactly as they do in the product. The wordmark is the one Fraunces 600.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (500, `clamp(40px, 4.6vw, 64px)`, 0.98, -0.035em): the hero H1 only, at most 16ch wide, balanced by line.
- **Headline** (500, `clamp(32px, 3.4vw, 46px)`, 1.08, -0.03em): section headlines, paired on desktop with a section copy column aligned to the baseline.
- **Gate title** (500, `clamp(34px, 3.4vw, 44px)`, 1, -0.03em): the three gate words, Fraunces in its single-word role.
- **Step title** (500, `clamp(26px, 2.4vw, 32px)`, 1.15, -0.02em): journey step headings, led by a 36px role pebble.
- **Document heading** (500, 24px, -0.02em): the packet's "Experience" heading; the only Fraunces inside an object.
- **Feature** (600, 20px, 1.3, -0.01em): the roster title and feature titles.
- **Lede** (400, `clamp(18px, 1.4vw, 21px)`, 1.5, soft ink): hero lede at 44ch and section copy at 48ch.
- **Copy** (400, 17px, 1.55): body, claim lines, installer labels, agent names at 600.
- **Object text** (400, 15px, 1.45): capability lines, notes, crew rows, browser fields, nav links at 600.
- **Label** (600, 14px/20px): buttons, segment choices, status lines, chat runtime at 700 and 13px.
- **Meta** (400, 14px, muted): runtime lines, pagers, readouts.
- **Fine** (400 or 600, 13px): chips, fine print, stage labels, the fictional marker, address bars.
- **Groove label** (11.5px, tabular): the 9:00 / This morning / 9:30 marks beneath a run groove.
- **Source path** (monospace, 12.5px, `teal-deep`): the quoted note's file and line.

### Named Rules

**The Fraunces Stays Up Rule.** Fraunces appears only at headline level and above, in the wordmark, the gate words and the packet's document heading. Nothing a visitor would click, and no object text, is set in Fraunces.

**The App Scale Rule.** Objects borrowed from the app (cards, trays, chips, beads, status lines) use the app's 14/13 meta scale; the marketing scale (display, headline, lede, 17px copy) is for prose around them, never inside them.

## Layout

One width: everything from the header to the footer sits in a 1184px page lane, so the page has one pair of margins and text blocks share one left edge. Gutters are 40px, falling to 20px under 640px. Sections are 128px apart (72px on phones), and each opens with a two-column section head (1.05fr / 0.95fr, headline left, one sentence of copy at most 34ch on the right, aligned at the baseline) above 1024px, stacking below. Journey steps are 0.8fr / 1.2fr with sticky copy at 96px and 96px between steps (72px under 1024px); the crew, FAQ, ownership and download sections use the same two-column split with 64px column gaps. The hero sets a one-line display headline over a supporting sentence beside three facts; Ownership is the page's one full field of ink (the inverse token, so it is a light band in dark mode); the footer is a deep-paper band that closes with the name at display size.

The hero roster is a tray holding a four-column grid at 12px gaps (two columns under 1024px, one under 640px), then a reserved "Waiting on you" row so the decision card can rise without moving the roster. The journey's stage track is sticky at the top of its section on paper with a hairline beneath, and shows only the current stage label on phones. The packet is a 20px sheet with a 360px review rail that stacks under 1024px; the browser panel, chat turn and small board are each single objects that keep their internal proportions down to a two-column board on phones. The header is a single 18px-padded row (12px on phones) with nav links that drop in order as width falls and the glazed Download always last.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is clay, not layering: a surface is either the flat paper ground, pressed into it (sunken), or raised from it (clay), lit from the top left in both themes. Flat panes (documents, sheets, chat replies, the theme menu) carry a 1px hairline ring and, for floating menus, the pop shadow; they are not clay. Hover lifts a pressable one pixel and widens its inner light; press sinks it to the sunken recipe and scales it to 0.97. Dark keeps the same recipes with the inner light reduced to a rim.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Clay** (`box-shadow: var(--clay)`): raised pressables at rest, agent cards, the decision card, installer rows, the active segment choice, the open FAQ trigger and the current-stage bead.
- **Clay hover** (`box-shadow: var(--clay-hover)`): hovered pressables, with the hover fill.
- **Sunken** (`box-shadow: var(--clay-sunken)`): trays, grooves, the stage track, beads at rest, chat threads, the segment track and the setup rule; also the pressed state of any clay control.
- **Glaze** (`box-shadow: var(--glaze)`): the one primary button per surface, with `--glaze-pressed` when active.
- **Sheet** (`box-shadow: var(--sheet)`): flat panes, a hairline ring with a 1px drop.
- **Pop** (`0 0 0 1px var(--border), var(--pop)`): the theme menu only.
- **Working ring** (`var(--clay), 0 0 0 2px plum at 35%`): an agent card while its agent executes.
- **Pebble** (`0 1px 2px ink 12%, inset top light, inset role tint`): identity orbs; the gate pebbles add a 6px paper halo so they can sit on the hairline rule.

### Named Rules

**The Two Levels Rule.** A surface holds at most two depth levels: a tray with clay objects in it, or paper with a sheet on it. Nothing is raised inside something that is raised.

**The Pressables Only Rule.** Clay volume belongs to things you press or pick up. Decorative containers, prose and documents stay flat; if an object cannot be acted on in the app, it is not clay here.

## Shapes

Corners are soft and a hair off symmetric. Buttons use the app's four-corner radius (11px 12px 11px 10px; 9px 10px 9px 8px at the small size) and installer rows 12px 13px 12px 11px, so the clay never looks machine-cut. Containers step up by role: chips and nav links 8px, claims and menus 10px, rows and documents 12px, clay cards 14px, sheets 16px, trays 18px, the packet 20px. Beads, pebbles, grooves, tracks and the setup rule are full pills or circles. Chat bubbles square one corner (4px) toward their author. Borders are a single 11% ink hairline; a 1.5px dashed line appears only on the fictional marker and the posting's hidden-instruction block. Hidden posting text is outlined in dashed plum, never filled. Focus is a 2px primary outline at 2–3px offset with a 6px radius.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Shape:** the app's off-symmetric button corners (11px 12px 11px 10px), 38px tall, 15px side padding, label type, 7px gap to a 16px icon; small is 32px with 9px 10px 9px 8px corners; icon size is 38px square.
- **Primary (glazed):** the glaze fill (indigo, lit top-left) with white text and the glaze shadow; hover brightens the fill; active uses the pressed glaze and scales to 0.97. One per surface.
- **Clay (secondary):** the clay fill with ink text and the clay shadow; hover lifts to the hover fill and shadow; active sinks to the sunken shadow. Used for See the packet, Request changes, Deny, the theme trigger and the mobile header Download.
- **Ghost:** transparent with muted text; hover tints with 6% ink and darkens to ink. Used for Replay and Ask again.
- **Focus:** a 2px primary outline at 2px offset on every button.

### Chips

- **Style:** 24px pills with 8px corners, 13px semibold text, 9px side padding; default is the sunken fill with soft ink.
- **State:** tones follow the bead law and the role colours: moss for Quoted, primary for Revised, ochre for a pending decision, plum for an executing runtime, teal for Scout, rose for failed. Chips never carry borders.

### Cards / Containers

- **Tray:** 18px corners, the sunken fill and shadow, 14px padding (12px in the roster, 8px on phones). Holds clay cards and lists; Ownership is not a tray but the page's one full field of ink.
- **Clay card:** 14px corners, clay fill and shadow, 16px/16px/14px padding; the agent card gains a 2px plum ring while working. Lists of cards inside a tray keep 12px gaps.
- **Sheet:** 16px corners, bright surface with the hairline ring; the packet sheet is 20px and clips its header and footer rows.
- **Document:** a flat 12px pane with a hairline ring inside the packet sheet; in dark it drops one step to surface so it stays a pane, not a light.

### Inputs / Fields

There are no text inputs on this page. The only form-like controls are the segment (platform tabs) and the FAQ accordion triggers, documented below.

### Navigation

- **Header:** brand pebble and Fraunces wordmark left; nav links in 15px semibold soft ink with 8px corners and a 6% ink hover tint; the clay theme trigger; then the glazed Download. Links drop by priority below 1024px, 768px and 640px; the mobile header keeps brand, theme and a 36px Download.
- **Theme menu:** a bright 10px pane with the pop shadow, 36px options with an 8px highlight tint and a primary check on the current choice; opens with a 180ms scale from 0.97, opacity only under reduced motion.
- **Stage track:** sticky on paper, a sunken 4px rail with a primary fill that scales from the left over 450ms; stage beads are small primary for completed, a large raised ringed bead for the current stage, and empty sockets ahead.
- **Segment:** a sunken 11px track with 3px padding; choices are 36px, 9px corners, muted label text; the active choice rises with the clay fill and shadow.

### Bead

A 14px pill (9px small, 18px large, 28px extra large) that is a sunken socket at rest. A tone fills it with a radial top-left light over the tone colour and a dark lower-right inset; the fill scales in from 0.8 over 220ms. A live bead adds a 2px outline of its tone at 30%. The current-stage bead is raised clay ringed by 1.5px primary.

### Pebble

A 36px circle (34–40px where used) filled with the role's soft tone and a top-left orb light, holding a 19px role icon or an initial in the role's tone at 700. Role tones: Scout teal, Writer plum, Reviewer moss, you ochre; roles without a tone fall back to the primary. Pebbles are identity only; they never indicate state.

### Groove with runs

A 14px-tall row with a 5px sunken channel; a run is an 11px plum pill positioned by start and end share of the morning, scaling in from the left over 1150ms, with a 1px muted "now" tick and 11.5px tabular labels beneath. Grooves show when an agent worked; the bead beside the status line shows the state.

### Installer row

A 72px clay row with 12px 13px 12px 11px corners, 17px semibold label with a 13px muted detail, and the size in primary beside a download icon; hover lifts one pixel, press sinks and scales to 0.985. It is the one place a card is itself the link.

### The rehearsal (signature motion)

Once per visit, when the roster is 35% in view, the page rewinds the server-rendered finished state to idle and plays five phases on a timer (idle 0ms, Scout 500ms, Writer 1750ms, Reviewer 3000ms, done 4250ms) by setting `data-phase` on the roster. Each phase lets CSS transitions carry the change: the working card gains its plum ring, its run scales along the groove, its status bead turns plum then primary, status text fades in with a 4px rise and 3px blur, and at done the Waiting on you title turns ochre and the decision card rises from 42% opacity over 420ms. Replay restarts it; reduced motion and no JavaScript show the finished state and never animate. All motion is transform and opacity on the app's strong ease-out (`cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`), with no keyframes.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** keep the page ground flat paper; put work in sunken trays and give clay volume only to pressables.
- **Do** follow the bead law exactly: plum executing, ochre waiting on you, primary completed, moss recorded positive, rose failed, empty socket idle, raised ringed bead for the current stage.
- **Do** place exactly one glazed indigo button per surface; make the second action clay and the third ghost.
- **Do** set every headline in Fraunces 500 with negative tracking and every object from the app in Source Sans 3 at the app's 14/13 scale.
- **Do** label fictional data with the dashed fictional marker wherever a job, packet or board appears.
- **Do** ship every transition as transform and opacity on the strong ease-out with a reduced-motion equivalent, and render the finished state on the server.
- **Do** keep light and dark as peers from the same tokens; test every tone in both.

### Don't:

- **Don't** raise an object inside a raised object, or stack more than two depth levels on a surface.
- **Don't** use a bead or chip tone for decoration, or ochre for anything that is not the visitor's decision.
- **Don't** put Fraunces on buttons, labels, chips or object text, or use any display face other than Fraunces.
- **Don't** add screenshots, testimonials, counts, ratings or logos; the product's own objects are the proof.
- **Don't** use keyframe animation, scale beyond press (0.97) or motion that moves layout; grooves, tracks and beads fill in place.
- **Don't** draw borders heavier than the 11% ink hairline except the dashed plum outline on hidden posting text and the dashed fictional marker.
