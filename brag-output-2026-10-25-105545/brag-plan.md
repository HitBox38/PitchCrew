# Pitchcrew — Your job search. A whole crew.

## Planning rubric
1. A local-first job-search app with AI role agents.
2. For job seekers preparing and tracking applications.
3. Turns profile experience and a job into a reviewed application packet.
4. Scout assesses fit; Writer drafts; Reviewer checks; the user approves exports.
5. Native raised clay cards, recessed controls and paper sheets are the visual identity.
6. Source: README.md, packages/ui and the approved fictional Demo workflow footage.
7. Use actual app recordings and favicon; no landing-page components or recreated UI.
8. Hook: the real application arrives as a physical object beside “Your job search. A whole crew.”
9. Keep the same Orbit Labs lead through assessment, drafting, source inspection, review and exact-document approval.

## Creative direction
Cinematic / epic, grounded in actual software. A virtual camera pushes toward decisions, tracks the relevant sheet, reveals source evidence and pulls back after approval. The UI itself keeps its native geometry and materials. Conventional arrow cursor remains inside the recording. Large explanatory copy tells a new viewer what Pitchcrew does.

Landscape 1920×1080, 30fps, 38 seconds. The longer duration preserves the full flow while adding readable explanations requested by the user. No narration requested; explanation is on screen. Previous cut preserved.

LOOK: ink stage #18242b; native paper #f2f1ec; warm white type #f2f1ec; sea-glass accent #b7d3cf. Fraunces Variable 600, headlines 70–76px; Source Sans 3 400/600, body 34px, labels 28px. App takes the right side in a 1060×820 viewport; close-ups preserve all native relative positions.

## Storyboard — 38s
- 0–5, Establish: actual board opens Orbit Labs. A 3D camera arrival settles. “Your job search. A whole crew.” / “AI agents prepare applications from your profile.”
- 5–10, Scout: camera moves toward Evaluate fit, then returns to the assessment. “Know the fit.” / “Scout compares each job with your profile.”
- 10–15.5, Writer: focus follows Shortlist and Draft application, then Writer’s running state. “Build your application.” / “Writer drafts your resume, cover letter and answers.”
- 15.5–20.5, Evidence: move from the actual resume into the native profile quotations. “Backed by your experience.” / “Claims link back to your profile notes.”
- 20.5–25.5, Reviewer: track Review packet, then the actual Reviewed state. “A second set of eyes.” / “Reviewer checks the packet before it reaches you.”
- 25.5–33, Approval: camera follows Request export approval into Inbox, then toward Approve packet. “You decide what leaves.” / “Review the exact documents. Approve before export.”
- 33–38, Resolution: pull back from the approval action to the actual Approved packet and manual/agent choices. “Your crew. Your call.” / “Board, profile, chats and packets are saved locally.”

Each explanatory sentence holds at least 3 seconds. Titles settle; no constant drifting. All camera motion has a purpose and is bounded; no click rings, giant cursor artwork, shake, fake UI or disconnected component collages. Macro 3D tilt is brief and ends flat for reading. Intentional close-up crops stay inside the app viewport.

## Audio revision
User feedback: music felt too serious. Replace orchestra with the lighter, laid-back Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol. 9 by Sascha Ende (ende.app), CC BY 4.0. Keep the cinematic picture, explanatory copy, full app flow and camera moves. Use the first 38 seconds at 0.34 gain with a short fade-in and 2.5-second fade-out. Rebuild the gentle stage illumination from this track’s RMS/bands; no new visual effects. Existing quiet camera accents stay restrained.

Music cue guidance: bundled preset estimates 114.84 BPM. Story and reading holds remain fixed; no recut needed for this music-only correction.
