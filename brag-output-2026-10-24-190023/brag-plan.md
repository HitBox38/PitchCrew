# Pitchcrew — one job, the whole crew

A new film, built from recorded interactions of the actual application. One fictional opportunity, Orbit Labs / Senior Frontend Engineer, remains the subject throughout.

Planning rubric: (1) Pitchcrew is a local-first job-search app whose crew prepares source-backed application packets. (2) It serves job seekers. (3) The useful outcome is a reviewed packet the user can approve. (4) Scout, Writer and Reviewer are separate visible roles. (5) Actual clay board cards, glazed buttons, recessed fields and paper sheets are the visual anchor. (6) Copy comes from app controls. (7) Source is packages/ui and the deterministic Demo adapter, never packages/landing. (8) The hook is an actual opportunity becoming a crew workflow. (9) The demonstrated flow is lead → fit assessment → shortlist → draft → source inspection → review → exact-packet approval.

Tone: polished, tactile, practical. Landscape 1920×1080. Target 32 seconds: longer than the default because the requested full flow needs readable actions and outcomes. No narration.

Hook: start on the real board and open Orbit Labs immediately. No landing-page title sequence.

Storyboard (32 seconds total):
- 0–3: Board → Orbit Labs. Header: “One job. Your whole crew.”
- 3–7: Click Evaluate fit, show Scout working and the resulting fit assessment. Label “01  Assess the fit”.
- 7–11: Shortlist, click Draft application, show Writer working then the packet. Label “02  Draft the application”.
- 11–17: Open Application / Documents, reveal the actual resume and profile citations. Label “03  Keep the evidence”.
- 17–20: Review packet, show Reviewer progress and Reviewed state. Label “04  Review the packet”.
- 20–28: Prepare export, request approval, open the corresponding Orbit Labs Inbox item, click Approve packet. Label “05  You approve”.
- 28–32: Hold the Approved state with Submit myself / Let an agent submit still visible. Header “Reviewed. Approved. Ready to apply.” Small Pitchcrew branding remains throughout.

All scenes are real interactions in one isolated fictional workspace. Waiting time may be shortened; never invent state changes. A normal black arrow with a white outline follows actual pointer coordinates. No decorative cursor artwork. Native app hover, sheet and clay motion remain enabled. No floating or manually aligned replica controls.

Visual identity: paper #f2f1ec; ink #2b2d31; indigo #3a5482; Source Sans 3 for small editorial labels; Fraunces Variable for brand. Native UI fills the film, with a restrained paper border and title strip. No recreated UI or flying component collages.

Audio: warm bundled Sascha Ende instrumental at low gain, sparse soft clicks at meaningful decisions. No added sound for every UI event. No audio-reactive deformation of UI. Beat guide: vol12 110BPM, emphasis near 7.09, 17.47, 22.93; readability takes precedence over beat alignment.

Share copy: “One job, a whole crew: Scout checks the fit, Writer drafts, Reviewer checks the packet. You approve what leaves Pitchcrew.”
