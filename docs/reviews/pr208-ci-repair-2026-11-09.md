A bounded passive dispatch ring identified the cause, rather than inferring it from the completion timeout:

1. Typing `p` inserted it at the intended `kind` value and moved the CodeMirror caret from 31 to 32.
2. A delayed controlled-value effect replaced the whole document with the older empty-kind value. Its `isolateHistory` annotation was `full`; the replacement mapped the caret to zero.
3. A second controlled update restored the document containing `p`, still with the caret at zero.
4. Typing `a` then produced `a{"version":1,"rules":[{"kind":"p"}]}`. Focus stayed in the editor; no matching completion could appear.

`useJsonEditor` now creates the editor and synchronizes controlled values/diagnostics in layout effects. Their order remains latest props → creation → initial synchronization. No typing delay, explicit completion shortcut or longer timeout was added.
