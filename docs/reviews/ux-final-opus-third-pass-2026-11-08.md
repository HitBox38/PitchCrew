I made no product, test or AGENTS edits, ran no tests or builds, did not poll the full check (session 26469), started no AI runs, and did not restart the daemon or clear caches. My tab is closed.

All measurements below were taken after confirming the actual `innerWidth`. Where a resize had not yet applied, I waited or navigated again before measuring, and I did not count resize lag as a product issue.

## Verdict

**Score: 9.0/10. PASS (just at the threshold).**

Every material finding from the earlier passes is fixed in the live app, including the second pass's one blocker (N1, the phone Decisions footer). I found no unresolved material flow issue. The items I found are wording, actor-label and focus polish (T1–T6 below). None of them blocks or misleads a user on the path from lead to applied.

This is an honest 9, not a comfortable one. Readability and the evidence I could not reproduce myself (busy, failure and recovery states) keep the score from going higher.

## Findings

### Material

None.

### Polish (recommended, not blocking)

- **T1: Raw actor ID "orchestrator" is shown to users.** Job details History lists rows such as "Recorded document verification findings — **orchestrator** · 2h ago" and "Moved to Drafting — **orchestrator**". The chat job-event chips carry the same raw ID in their tooltip and accessible name ("orchestrator · 11/8/2026, 8:22:59 PM"). The Activity page already maps this actor to "Pitchcrew" (`ActivityPage/constants.ts:39`), but `components/CardDetails/components/JobHistory.tsx:22` and `ChatView/components/JobEventChips.tsx:16` call `nameOf(actor)` without that map. Fix: share `actorLabels` (or a small `actorName` helper in `lib/`) in both places.
- **T2: "User recorded a manual submission" is the only history row in the third person.** It sits among "Moved to …"/You rows on Live UX Studio. The text is a stored event message produced at `orchestrator/src/crew/cards.ts:55`. Fix: render a display sentence for this event in the UI, for example "You recorded that you applied". Do not rewrite stored events.
- **T3: Two names for one state in Decisions.** The handoff status chip reads "**Ready for you to submit**" while the stage, rail, Board and chat all say "**Ready to apply**". Suggest using "Ready to apply" for the chip too.
- **T4: Phone Packet sheet close returns focus to Browser, not Packet.** At 390, after closing the Packet sheet with ×, `document.activeElement` was the header's **Browser** button. The expected target is the Packet toggle that owns the sheet. I observed this once, with a pointer click. Low impact for pointer users, but a keyboard user would land on the wrong control.
- **T5: The reason behind the fit score is far down the phone lead sheet.** On the Anthropic lead at 390, "Crew assessment" starts about 5,300px down, after the full posting. The footer shows only "Fit 60/100". Suggest showing the assessment (or a one-line reason) above the description, or linking to it from the fit line.
- **T6: "Approved" is used for the Reviewer's agreement.** Saved chat replies say "I approved the packet", and History shows "Reviewer approved the packet: …". Pitchcrew reserves approval for the user's gate. This is stored Demo/runtime output, so the parent should check whether current prompts still produce the phrasing; if they do, prefer "I passed the packet" or "Review passed".

### Parent decisions, assessed independently

- **I've submitted without a confirmation modal or Undo toast.** I agree it is acceptable. The label, the "only after you have applied" hint and "Nothing has been sent" appear together on every surface, and the parent showed it can be reversed through Board → Move to…. An Undo toast would still be a nice addition.
- **Phone Board stage selector plus heading.** Acceptable. The heading carries the count, sort and focus target.
- **Non-modal Packet split without Escape.** Acceptable. Close is reachable on desktop and phone. See T4 for the focus-return detail.
