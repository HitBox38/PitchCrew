# Job details

The job sheet has three sections: Job, Application and Activity. Its header takes two identity rows (company · location · salary above the title, with Edit job, original posting and close) and one progress row: a compact bead track naming only the current step (all six steps on hover, "Stage N of 6" for screen readers), live agent pebbles, and the ratings written on one 0–100 scale as "Fit 82 · Packet match 74". Closed jobs show their status beside an empty socket where the track would be (a closed job is not failed work). Work in progress is said once, in the dock, so the header has no work-status pill or "Next:" line.

The **Now dock** replaces the footer as the sheet's one raised surface. It states whose move it is in one sentence (with a second line for the reason, the runtime that will run, or the captured evidence) and holds the controls that act on that step, chosen by `next-action.ts` over the full precedence order and phrased by `dock.ts`. It is recessed while an agent, the queue or the employer has the move, raised with at most one glazed action when the move is the user's, and ochre-tinted for a question or decision waiting on the user (an agent's question, an uncertain submission, approving files, choosing how to apply, and, by the header's and ledger's rule, a brief to confirm or files ready to apply); the shadow tokens share one structure, so the change morphs. A failed run stays visible on a rose line beside the still-valid next action. Each section has its own reading position inside the sheet's main work area; the header and dock never scroll, and the sections' hairline appears only once content scrolls under it.

Opening a job immediately shows its identity header and a skeleton while the sheet code or full job source loads. The loading status is announced to assistive technology, skeletons respect reduced motion, and the sheet can be closed while loading. Source failures keep the header visible and offer Retry before enabling the full details and workflow actions.

Stage changes and agent launches register per-job pending state synchronously, shared with the board card; the pressed action and header show progress through HTTP completion and snapshot refresh. Reserved launches keep their spinner until a run appears. Queued, paused, failed and waiting work use resting indicators; View queued action opens Queue, and required questions retain Answer in job chat. Failures clear pending state and restore the available actions.

## Information and decisions

Job opens with Scout's first substantive note when the job is scored (a note that only restates what the score means joins the rest behind **All N notes**; the header carries Fit), then the original description as readable prose (its saved text unchanged, list lines hanging under their text) and tags. Application's Packet match to job section offers **Reevaluate with packet** in every board stage, including submitted and closed jobs, once a packet and current extracted brief exist. It queues an isolated Scout assessment using the complete resume, cover letter, form answers, note, claims, structured job brief and Profile evidence. Active work, pending questions, workspace capacity and runtime/account gates retain their usual protections; brief edits must be saved first.

Reevaluation refreshes candidate fit and saves a separate advisory 0–100 packet match rating with reasons, candidate-fit reasoning, evaluation time, run ID and exact packet digest/context revision. The rating measures requirement coverage, relevance, consistency and tailoring; it is not a hiring probability or packet approval. Status, documents, review feedback, independent review evidence and approvals remain intact. Packet or adopted brief changes, including corrected job details, clear the current rating while retaining historical events. Cancellation, missing/invalid ratings and changed inputs never publish a replacement score. User questions release capacity and resume the same isolated evaluation with saved answer provenance.

Posting provenance lives in a secondary hairline disclosure. Application is a ledger of the brief and requirements, documents, reviews and export: one line per step with its bead and status, the current step open by default (following the job while untouched) and any number open at once. Confirmed briefs show their compact summary with one quiet row of tools (Edit brief · Compare with posting · Extract again…; Compare appears once a brief exists or is being written), and the "can’t tell if the posting is complete" line appears only while a brief is being checked or edited; a job without an application link offers **Add application link**, which opens Edit job; a manual form choice is stated once rather than beside "not checked". Deep-review reports load only when Reviews first opens. The body has no glazed or raised buttons: Edit brief, Extract again…, Add requirement, Inspect application form, Deep review resume, Writing guide and Reevaluate are quiet, and the dock holds the step's one glazed action. Headings inside a ledger row (and Packet match) are 14/600 like the row titles. The brief opens with one muted line ("Check it against the posting: Pitchcrew can’t tell if its saved copy is complete.") with Compare with posting at its end. Before drafting, Job shows Scout's read; afterwards Writer and Reviewer notes appear where they apply. Each ledger step says who holds it, matching the header and dock: an agent's question turns that step ochre ("Reviewer asked you") and a live turn plum ("Writer is working"), mapped by what the run works on (extraction → Brief, drafting → Documents, review and deep review → Reviews; fit and packet rating claim no step). A brief that doesn't exist yet reads "Not prepared", and Reviews reads "Not reviewed" before there is a packet. A step turns ochre only when its own decision waits on the user (a brief to confirm, exact files to approve), by the same rule as the header's track, so a lead or a brief not yet extracted shows a neutral ring. A closed job has no current step. While the dock offers Extract brief, the brief column shows only the agent choice; otherwise it uses the dock's words (Extract brief, Write it myself). A lead's dock asks "Worth applying?" with one glazed action and quiet alternatives. When Reviewer requests changes, Documents reads "Revision needed", Reviews reads "Changes requested" and lists "Reviewer asked for", both open by default, and the dock offers **Revise application** with the request count, quoting the first request, plus a quiet **Read requests** that opens Reviews. An agreed or approved packet whose review no longer matches the brief reads "Review again" in Reviews and the dock says to review the packet again; there is no separate warning banner, and during review the dock already names the step. Reviews ends with a Deep review block (heading, launch button, one line) whose saved reports appear below it, with history only for two or more reports. The dock requests export approval for exactly the files it names ("PDF, formatted, plus Markdown copies"); Change files opens the format and layout controls in Application's Export step.

The next action prioritizes current work, pending user questions and uncertain submissions. Dock actions do what they say rather than switching tabs, and when a step happens elsewhere the button names the place (Open in Decisions, Open job chat): an unavailable brief is extracted by the application agent (Write it myself opens the editor), a linked form is inspected by a form-capable agent (I’ll check it myself opens the manual choice), outcomes after applying are recorded with the dock's own buttons (a menu on phones; none is glazed, and recording never contacts the employer) except on an offer, whose only change is withdrawing: its dock rests with a quiet Add a lesson and Activity keeps Mark withdrawn, and an uncertain submission with captured confirmation is verified or rejected from the dock, which quotes the page, while Other evidence opens the receipt and its outside-verification form. Drafting confirmation is offered only while the brief is on screen; elsewhere the dock says Review brief, which opens the brief ready to confirm (its editors and the whole form list), and the dock then offers Confirm brief. A lead with an older score but no brief offers Shortlist first, with Extract brief beside it. A current extracted brief supports fit evaluation; drafting requires confirmation and personal answers. Drafting uses the isolated application draft endpoint with the chosen application agent. Packet review, exact-document export approval in Decisions, choosing how to apply, agent preparation and recording an actual manual submission remain separate states; recording is offered only once files are saved. Runtime/account details appear on the dock's second line beside launch actions, and unavailable agents explain what needs attention. Quiet alternatives that start work or move the job (Extract brief, Evaluate again, Shortlist, Record manual submission) are disabled while a request is in flight, like the glazed action; navigation stays available.

An agent working on another job or standalone conversation does not disable this job's launch actions. The same agent can run across independent queues within the workspace concurrency limit; active work and pending questions on this job retain their existing protections.

Activity contains submission receipts first, a 48px Status row when an outcome can be recorded by hand and the dock doesn't record it (lead, shortlisted, changes requested, offer: the track's word for the stage and a quiet **Change…** menu that records outcomes only, such as Mark withdrawn; workflow and review moves stay with the dock and crew), external application registration, lessons, tracking evidence and saved history as a compact one-line timeline on done beads, with system steps attributed to Pitchcrew. Saved history loads from the session-protected `/api/cards/:id/history` route (the latest 200 job events, without event payloads) and merges recent run events from the workspace snapshot, so older jobs keep their history after it leaves the snapshot's latest 100 events. Uncertain submission outcomes open their evidence rather than suggesting another submission. Recording a status never contacts an employer or grants export/submission permission.

## Editing a job

Edit job opens the same form used by Add job, prefilled with company, title, posting URL, location, salary, full description and tags. Fetch from link can fill supported public postings for review; editing never offers the Scout action that creates another job. Application registration remains a separate action in Activity. Cancel, Escape, outside dismissal and navigation protect unsaved edits, and failed saves keep the form open with its draft. Saving updates the existing job and refreshes its details and board card.

The user-session-only `PUT /api/cards/:id` endpoint accepts the form fields and the opened job's `expectedUpdatedAt`. Stale versions, owned jobs, active attached runs and uncertain submissions block writes. Posting changes invalidate the application brief, candidate fit, packet match rating, packet review, form assessments and unused export/browser approvals in one transaction. URL or description edits clear old captured markup so extraction uses the edited posting. Tags alone preserve reviewed evidence and approvals; identical saves append no events. Status, application tracking, user lessons, packet files, discovery provenance and history remain attached to the same job. Agent job-correction permissions remain limited to company, title, location and salary.

Save or discard other job-detail drafts before opening the posting editor. Its fields remain stable while workspace polling continues; conflicting changes require reopening the editor.

## Live agents and job chat

A waiting agent's question is quoted in the dock; **Answer here** opens the chat's own question form inside the dock (its draft is shared with the chat), and **Answer in job chat** stays beside it until then. While the form is open the dock keeps only what happens next, and the × (**Hide answer**) folds the form away without cancelling the question. While Application is open, **Compare with posting** shows the saved posting beside the brief (above it on narrow sheets), widening the sheet on wide screens. On phones the progress row folds away once the reading column scrolls and returns at the top; identity and tabs stay. A deep review's dock line names the review ("Deep review · Writer is working"). The dock never claims more than it knows: a wait without a pending question offers **Open job chat** rather than an answer, a job left in drafting with no run says no agent is drafting, a launch blocked by setup says **Finish setup to continue** and names the fix, and an uncertain submission without captured confirmation offers **Check submission evidence**.

Active workflow and attached conversation runs appear as named agent pebbles in the header's progress row; their status is spoken to assistive technology and shown in the dock. Saved and streamed activity merge by run ID. Hover and keyboard focus show their public current activity, latest commentary and elapsed time. Clicking an avatar opens its existing conversation at `/chat/<thread>?run=<run-id>` and focuses the matching turn. Touch users can open the same conversation directly. Administrative seat reservations are omitted; a deep-review coordinator is replaced by its running or waiting workers from the same review report, even when their shared root points to an earlier conversation turn. Answered waits are replaced only by their explicit active continuation chain. Independent runs retain separate links, including runs of the same role or different agents sharing a name; repeated names gain conversation and turn labels. Waiting agents show Waiting for you; once the question is answered and before its continuation starts they show Answer saved, and the footer no longer offers Answer in job chat. Opening chat never cancels a run.

Ordinary application workflows and source extraction use the same bounded, sanitized activity channel as chat and deep-review stages. Source extraction joins the existing application conversation or creates one. Provider activity retains at most 80 entries and 4,000 characters per commentary entry; private reasoning bodies and raw tool payloads are excluded. Completed activity is saved on the run and included in indexed conversation history. Compact streams overlay only active runs and a terminal handoff, keeping saved history paged. Run-targeted links load the relevant history page and continue following live work when it is the latest page.

## Drafts and accessibility

Sections mount on first visit and remain mounted afterward. Collapsed editors use the shared accordion's mounted panels, so tab and disclosure changes preserve drafts. Brief edits, lesson text, tracking identifiers, email thread drafts, external submission fields and external verification evidence participate in the sheet's unsaved-changes guard. Closing, leaving the page or following a job-chat link requires saving or explicitly discarding pending edits. Selecting another section does not discard them.

The sheet uses Base UI dialog semantics, focus management, tabs and accordions, with the same shared materials and reduced-motion treatment as the rest of the app. Recessed segmented tabs, a paper reading column with hairline sections, a sans job title on the title token, quiet agent pebbles and the raised Now dock preserve the app's material identity within the compact layout. The dock's sentence settles in with the shared `clay-settle-in` motion when the step changes. On phones the tabs fill the width, dock actions stretch to 44px targets and the dock accounts for safe areas; Electron's existing title-bar bounds apply to the sheet and backdrop.

Regression coverage includes initial reading layout, mobile bounds, draft preservation/save/close behavior, live workflow previews, turn-targeted chat navigation, saved turn replay, source-extraction progress and existing deep-review/board flows. Provider calls remain outside automated verification.

## Form requirements before drafting

For a linked job, the prerequisite-aware dock leads to **Inspect application form** before Draft application, running the inspection directly when a form-capable agent is enabled. The brief section lets the user select an enabled agent with browser and form assessment tools and opens its attached inspection conversation. On return the form appears once, inside the brief: a summary line (field count, flagged-required count, page, when checked), then only what changes the user's preparation (blockers, answers the form needs, files with their formats, length limits, and what wasn't checked, including hidden, disabled or unassessed controls), then every field in page order behind **Show all N fields**. The list is open while the brief is being edited for confirmation, since confirming adopts every field, and folded otherwise. Fields say Required only when the control is flagged; nothing is called optional. **Inspect again** sits beside the heading (a short menu when several agents can inspect). The user confirms these findings with the complete brief. If inspection is unavailable, an explicit manual-check checkbox allows an uninspected packet while keeping that limitation visible. Jobs without application links explain the missing inspection.

Writer and Reviewer receive the confirmed summary in their isolated context and card tool. A changed inspection requires new confirmation and packet review; old approvals cannot authorize the changed requirements. Exact browser action approval, export and final submission remain separate; browser actions may be approved automatically within the agent's browser mode, while export and the marked final submission always need the user's decision (recognizing unmarked sending is best effort; see [computer use](computer-use.md)).

## Contributor rules

Job details offers Edit job in its header, reusing the Add job form with prefilled posting fields and tags, unsaved-change protection and stale-write checks. User posting edits share the existing active-work/uncertain-submission gates and evidence/approval invalidation; tag-only edits preserve reviews. Job details uses a two-row identity header with one progress row, Job/Application/Activity sections and the prerequisite-aware Now dock (see above), whose actions act or name the place they open. Its Application section shows packet match only once a packet (or an earlier rating) exists, offering queued whole-packet reevaluation through an isolated Scout at every board stage once a packet and current extracted brief exist; a separate advisory 0–100 packet match rating retains reasons and exact packet/context provenance. Reevaluation preserves status, workflow feedback, packet review and approvals; packet or adopted context/source changes clear the current rating. Visited sections and collapsed editors stay mounted to preserve drafts; closing, routing and job-chat links use the combined unsaved-changes guard. Header agent avatars preview public current turn activity on hover/focus and open the exact existing conversation with a run-targeted link. Ordinary workflow and source-extraction turns share bounded, sanitized live activity and paged saved chat history; extraction joins the job conversation. Deep-review reports load on first disclosure. Export setup stays in Application, requested from the dock, and Reviews opens with the current packet's Reviewer summary and Writer change notes. See [docs/job-details.md](job-details.md). Stage changes and agent launches synchronously register per-job pending actions in the workspace store, so sheet buttons, header status and board cards show feedback before HTTP completes. Pending indicators survive snapshot reloads and hand off to reserved startup, running, queued or held states; initial source loading keeps the job identity visible with a skeleton.
