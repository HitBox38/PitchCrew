# Home

Rules for contributors and coding agents. If a change makes anything here false, update this file in the same change.

Home (`/`, view `home`, title "Home", ⌘/Ctrl+1) is the start page. It holds one ranked **Needs you** list of every attention item (CONTEXT.md "Attention item") with the top item open and decided in place, beside the crew's live work and what was recorded since the user was last here. The board is Pipeline at `/pipeline` ([Pipeline](board.md)); its Needs you deck, tracking review strip and setup checklist moved here. The sidebar's primary places are Home ⌘1, Pipeline ⌘2, Crew ⌘3 and Profile ⌘4. Decisions moved under More and keeps `/inbox`, its history and deep links ([Decisions](inbox-redesign.md)). There is no `/board` route. The command palette lists Home ("What waits on you and what the crew is doing") and Pipeline ("Jobs by stage", also found by "board" and "kanban") first. Page code lives in `packages/ui/src/HomePage/` and its materials in `packages/ui/src/styles/home.css`. Why Home replaced the board as the start page is recorded in [ADR 0006](adr/0006-home-is-the-needs-you-desk.md).

## The attention list

`attentionItems(data, { remoteRequests })` in `packages/ui/src/lib/attention.ts` returns every item, ranked. It is pure and zod-free and reads no clock. Each item has a stable key `{kind}:{entityId}` (the row key and the `?item=` value), a tier, the job, the asking agent, the conversation and queue it concerns, when the user's move began (`askedAt`, empty when unknown), an expiry for tier 1, what it blocks, a tone (ochre, or rose only for failed work) and how it is decided. `useAttention()` shares one result between the shell and Home through `cachedAttention`, which recomputes only when a snapshot slice the model reads changes; the array is shared, so never sort or change it in place. The words for an item (`attentionSubject`, `attentionKindLabel`, `attentionAsk` and `attentionPhrase`) live in `packages/ui/src/lib/attention-labels.ts`, so no view words one item two ways. A failed or interrupted request's ask is a possessive noun phrase: "Scout's fit evaluation stopped before finishing", "Writer's follow-up for Reviewer stopped before finishing", "Writer's reply to “Compare the offers” was interrupted when Pitchcrew stopped"; once nothing stopped is left in a queue that stays paused, "Paused until you resume the queue".

A preparation counts as a packet item only when its delivery is missing, completed or cancelled, or paused in a queue the user stopped ("Preparation stopped"); moving preparation is crew work, a waiting one is a question and a failed one a failure. An uncertain submission is a verify item, never a packet stage. A stale review is crew work and missing answers are a brief item, so neither makes an export item.

**Promotion and de-duplication.** A brief with a missing or stale form or no confirmation moves to tier 2 and blocks its queue while its job queue is `ready` or `blocked` and the queue's next request is a draft, review or deep review. Promotion does not wait for `blocked`: with every slot busy the drain skips that queue without recording a reason, so it stays `ready` until a slot frees. Missing answers alone never promote, because they gate export, not drafting. A `blocked` queue is dropped only when its saved reason is exactly one of the fixed server messages (the three brief and form messages, "Verify the previous submission outcome first." and the message about finishing an approved Profile update), copied verbatim with their source lines in `attention.ts`, and the item that explains it exists. Every other hold stays, with its reason verbatim. If the server's wording changes, both items show; a hold is never hidden.

**Ranking.** Tier first; inside tier 1 the soonest expiry (unknown last); then the oldest `askedAt` (unknown last); then the key (`attentionOrder`).

**Connection requests** are the one kind that is not saved: a pending request lives in daemon memory for ten minutes. It comes from the notification center's single `/api/remote-mcp` reader (`useNotificationStore().remoteRequests`); never fetch it again, since tests assert the read count. Its row is named by the app-provided name, quoted (its kind label says "Connection request"), only links to Settings and states the expiry ([external MCP](external-mcp.md)).

## One count

`attentionCounts(items)` derives every Needs you count from the one list. Home's ochre sidebar badge ("Home, N waiting on you"), its title fact and the Needs you heading chip always show the same `home` count. `crew` (role and skill proposals and instruction updates), `profile` (Profile updates, including one stuck applying) and `skills` (under More) equal the formulas the sidebar used before, so a crew proposal counts on Home and on Crew. `failed`, the rose items, equals the app-bar queue chip's failed count (`queueAttention`) on the same snapshot. Decisions keeps its own pager over its four kinds and has no shell badge; the bell and the queue chip are unchanged.

Accepted divergence: board card beads, sidebar job rows, the job details dock and Decisions still read the four-kind `decisions()`, `jobAsks()` and `jobSignals()` in `packages/ui/src/ClayInterface/signals.ts`. They can show waits Home does not list (user-approved browser actions still executing, briefs on applied jobs) and miss kinds Home lists. Rebasing them on the attention list is a follow-up; never derive a Needs you count from them.

## Deciding in place

Invariant 1 is unchanged: Home renders the existing deciding components, which post their existing gated endpoints. Home adds no endpoint, approval path, optimistic decision, Approve all or Undo, and its code spells no API path; `proposalAct` (`packages/ui/src/ChatWork/helpers.ts`), `guideChange` (`packages/ui/src/WritingSettings/helpers.ts`) and `queueMove` (`packages/ui/src/QueuePage/helpers.ts`) build the calls for proposal cards, writing suggestions and Up next's reorder. `deskAction({ item, data })` (`packages/ui/src/HomePage/records.ts`, given the snapshot the row was drawn from) wraps the store's `action`: it forwards the path, method, body and options unchanged, so what is sent never changes. A call whose item leaves the list is announced by its record line and the desk's live region, so its success toast is dropped. A call that leaves the item in place makes no record: the row itself changes (an approved packet then reads "Choose how to apply", which its trigger names as it takes focus back), and a component that sends its own success toast, such as Refresh comparison on a tracking review, keeps it. Either way each decision is announced once; error toasts stay. Home passes it, never the raw action, to every embedded deciding component; Up next's reorder is not a decision and moves the queue through Queue's own `useQueueOrder` with the store's action. An item whose decision needs a full form opens it, and opening it decides nothing.

## Record lines

A decision made on Home leaves a record line with its consequence where the row stood: a filled primary bead (moss for Screening, Interviewing and Offer), the subject and muted outcome text that is never cut. From 600px of desk it is one line on the rows' grid; on a narrower desk it takes a closed row's shape, the outcome under the subject across the row, and a long subject ends in an ellipsis. Records live in memory for the app session; they survive route changes but not a reload. A row whose call is in flight stays where it is, drawn from its last view, even when a poll snapshot lacks it. After a successful call a record is made only if the item's key has left the fresh list; a decision that moves the item in place, such as approving a packet (it becomes Choose how to apply), makes none, and the row itself shows the new step. There is at most one record per key per session: later calls on the key amend it (a Skip's trailing queue resume), and deciding a key that came back replaces the old record. A trailing call the daemon refuses amends it as well, marked `failed`, so the record says what did not happen while the workspace shows the error; one saved while only the reload failed counts as done. A record says only what completed: a Skip whose removal emptied the queue reads "Skipped the fit evaluation" until its resume succeeds, then "· the job's queue continues", or "· the job's queue is still paused" when the resume was refused. Records render under the React key `record:{key}:{decidedAt}`, never the live item's key. A call saved while the reload failed stays pending until a snapshot without the item arrives (then it is recorded) or for 10 seconds (then it counts as moved in place), and a later call on the key supersedes it: chat stream updates also change the store while still carrying the old snapshot, so the next change alone proves nothing. Any other error records nothing, and the component shows its own error. A brief is the one decision made in job details that still counts as made on the desk: its button opens job details at Application ("Check form and confirm" while a linked form is unchecked, then "Answer and confirm" or "Review and confirm"), and once the snapshot shows it confirmed at its current revision and its row has left, it records "Brief confirmed · Writer and Reviewer see only what you confirmed" (`watch` and `release` in `records.ts`; a watch ends 10 seconds after job details closes). Other items resolved elsewhere (in chat, job details, Decisions, by an agent or by expiry) leave without a record. The words state what did and did not happen, for example "Approved: {summary}. {Agent} carries it out; you verify the outcome after." for a final submission, "Rejected: nothing was done. {Agent}'s run may ask again or end.", "Retry queued · 2nd in line", "Skipped the fit evaluation · the job's queue continues", "Resumed the job's queue · 3rd in line", "Handed to {Agent} to prepare · nothing is submitted until you approve the final step" and "Marked withdrawn · nothing is sent to {company}". An answer's record reads its own continuation (the request the answer queued, by `continuationRequestId`), never its queue's state alone, since a queue reads running while another run in it works: "{Agent} continues now" only once that delivery runs (or its queue is about to start), "continues after you answer {Agent}'s question" while another question waits in the queue (the server starts nothing there until it is answered), "continues when {Agent}'s run ends" while another run holds the queue, "{Agent}'s continuation is on hold" in a paused or blocked queue, else its place in the waiting line.

## Keyboard, focus and guards

Row keys are Home's own, because Base UI 1.8's accordion has no arrow-key handling. An `onKeyDown` on the desk, never on `window`, moves between visible row triggers with ArrowUp and ArrowDown (wrapping) and Home and End while focus is on a trigger, and with j and k from anywhere inside the desk, except while typing (`typing()` in `packages/ui/src/shortcuts.ts`, which counts anything inside a form), with a modifier, or while a dialog or menu is open. Triggers inside a closed fold or hidden panel are skipped. The open question row passes `active` to `QuestionAnswerForm` only while focus is inside the row, and `answerOnSubmit`, so its 1–9 option keys act only while focus is inside its answer form, never from the page body or the row's trigger (which takes focus after each decision), and, like a click, only select a suggestion.

Control/Command+Enter presses the open row's glazed verb through `useDecideShortcut` (`packages/ui/src/hooks/useDecideShortcut.ts`): the verb-specific approval on a browser row ("Approve click", "Approve typing"…), Retry on a failure row and Apply update on a tracking row. While a failed follow-up waits, the stopped request offers no Retry, and the follow-up's own Retry and Skip carry no shortcut. The shortcut fires only while focus is inside the open row and that row contains the button, so a kept-mounted closed row never fires. It never fires from a field: native inputs, selects, text areas, editable text and the ARIA field roles combobox, listbox, option, textbox, searchbox, spinbutton, slider, checkbox, radio and switch, which makes Tracking's Application select a field. Never give the desk, a tier or a row one of those roles. It also ignores repeats, composition, a key another handler already used and any press within 800 ms (`decideArmMs`) of the row opening. **A final submission never gets the shortcut.** After each decision focus lands inside the next open row, so a repeated press could otherwise reach one. `isFinalSubmission` (`packages/ui/src/ComputerApprovals/helpers.ts`) names both forms the server treats as a submission: an action marked `purpose: 'submission'` (Approve submission) and a dialog decision that continues an uncertain submission through `submissionAttemptId` (Approve answer). Home passes no shortcut scope for either, `BrowserApprovalActions` ignores a string scope on either as well, and the record line for either adds "you verify the outcome after". Decisions has no shortcut for browser approvals, and chat's tray keeps its own beside the live page. Packets, briefs, questions and proposals have no shortcut. A verb with the shortcut carries `aria-keyshortcuts`, a title and the quiet `DecideHint`, which is hidden on coarse pointers, below 48rem and below 640px of the row's `decide` container.

After a desk decision the next live item in rank order opens, its fold first, and its trigger takes focus with `preventScroll`, never its buttons, so a second Enter cannot decide. The page scrolls only when the next verbs are off screen (`block: 'nearest'`, clear of the stuck tier caption through `scroll-padding-top`; a tier's first row rests 14px higher, by its own caption, so the group above leaves whole and no caption is cut); with nothing left, the closing line takes focus. A decision that ends in a dialog or sheet (Not applying's confirmation, or job details opened for verify, export or a brief) refocuses the open row's trigger one frame after the call settles or job details closes, unless the user moved focus meanwhile or another dialog, sheet or menu holds it; Base UI alone returns focus to the last element still on the page, which can be a sidebar link. For 800 ms after a deciding click, a capture-phase listener drops clicks within 12px of it that land in the newly opened row's body; when the open row leaves without a decision here (resolved elsewhere, or expired with its run), every click in the next row's body is held for 800 ms, since it was aimed at the row that left. Keyboard activations are never held back. One polite live region announces "{record text}. Next: {subject}, {kind label}." `?item=<key>` opens and focuses that row, opening its fold, then clears itself with a replacing navigation; it works the same while Home is already open (an in-app link), and a key that no longer waits is cleared without opening anything. Rows visited during the visit stay mounted, so drafts survive closing them. An item that changes tier moves and stays open. If ranking moves the open row past its tier's third row into a closed fold (an approved packet is dated by its approval, so it sorts last; a promoted brief can land in a full tier), that fold opens so the row stays in view and keeps focus; a fold the user closes over the open row stays closed. An item resolved elsewhere leaves, the first live item opens if it was the open one, and focus moves only if it was inside.

## The crew rail

The rail is a `<section aria-label="Crew at work">`, not an `aside`: pages render inside `<main>`, where a complementary landmark fails axe. It is a view over job and conversation queues; scheduling stays per job ([ADR 0002](adr/0002-schedule-work-by-job.md)), and Home never lines work up by agent. Slots belong to lanes, not runs: several runs in one queue use one slot, and the snapshot does not say which queues hold slots, so `active of concurrency` is the authority and any surplus over running queues and background routines reads "{k} starting".

- **Working now** ("{active} of {concurrency} work slots in use") has one row per executing run, ordered by start, with the chat stream's live activity overlaid (`liveRunIndex`), and "Follow-up for Reviewer" as the action and "Writer for Reviewer" as the agent of a crew follow-up (never the follow-up's own instruction, which would not fit). A request's action is worded by `requestLabel` (`packages/ui/src/lib/run-labels.ts`), which Up next shares: a board action's label, "Continue after your answer" for the request an answer queues (its text is the agent's own instruction, never shown), "Summarize before continuing", a scheduled routine's name, else the user's words cut at a word to fit two narrow lines. Where a job and its action do not fit on one line (a long company in a narrow rail), the action moves under the job and wraps at its words, two lines at most; a rail row never cuts its action inside a word, and a job too long for the rail ends in an ellipsis. Durations drop a zero remainder ("1 h", not "1 h 0 min"). A run waiting on a browser approval shows a static ochre bead, "Waiting for your OK" as its step and "waiting {age}", and leads to its Needs you row; others show a breathing plum bead and their duration and open the job, or the conversation of a job-free queue. A running queue held only by a review-seat reservation reads "Reserved for {company}'s deep review", never a working agent, and background routines get their own line. A browser request's end is written "about HH:MM" (run start plus `runtimeTimeLimit` from `@pitchcrew/core/run-limits`; workflow runs set their deadline a little later), never "no later than".
- **Up next** lists ready queues in workspace order with the places `readyLine` (`packages/ui/src/lib/ready-line.ts`) gives them, the same places the desk's record words, the Pipeline table and Crew's lens read. A queue that already holds its slot reads "Starting" and takes no free one: a launch under way (the drain reserves the delivery, and the slot, before the run exists) or a crew follow-up queued by a completed run, whose chain keeps the slot until the follow-up starts (a restart or a runtime hold drops the chain, so such follow-ups hold slots only up to those no running queue, routine or launch explains). Of the rest, the first `concurrency − active` read "Starting", then "Next in line", "2nd in line" and so on; a queue behind a promoted brief reads "Waits on you · brief", links to that item and takes no place, and a queue whose next request its agent cannot start (paused, its runtime missing, a board action on a runtime that cannot isolate application work, or waiting for sign-in: `headStartBlock`) is left out, because the server never gives it a slot (Crew's lens and the table show it held). Move to top, up and down come from `QueueOrderMenu`, with moves computed over the whole ready line (`queueMoves`). The first three rows show, plus every queue released from the desk this session wherever it stands; each other stretch folds into "N more waiting · A, B and N more", which opens in place to at most eight rows and then "N more in Queue", and a stretch of two rows or fewer is drawn in full. The heading counts every queue in the line, brief-held ones included. Rows open the job (or the conversation), a brief-held row opens its brief, and user-paused queues add "Paused by you · n", linking to Queue.
- The **crew line** links to Crew with the working agents' pebbles, `crewSummary` and "{Agent} on {k} jobs".
- **Pipeline** shows open jobs, Leads with "{k} not scored", Shortlisted, In progress and Applied, with Closed set apart. Stage rows are plain counts, since Pipeline has no stage filter to link to; "{k} not scored" links to `/pipeline?mode=table&view=not-scored` and Open pipeline to `/pipeline`.

## Since you were last here

The baseline is the device-local `pitchcrew-home-seen-v1`, a plain ISO time; every read and write tolerates storage failure. Home reads it once on mount and keeps it for the visit, so lines never vanish while the user reads. It keeps the last moment Home was visible and focused (`seenTracker`): while Home is in front it writes the current time every 60 seconds and when focus or visibility returns, and once more the moment Home stops being in front (blur, the page hiding, `pagehide` or unmount). An event while Home is already out of the user's sight writes nothing, so a later hide or unload of a window the user had left, or of a Home mounted hidden, never moves the mark; a visible but unfocused window does not count as here. Browser and Electron keep separate values; a shared marker would need a new preference or event version. `sinceWindow` (`packages/ui/src/lib/since.ts`) turns it into the window: none or unreadable means the last 24 hours ("In the last 24 hours"), anything older than seven days is capped ("In the last 7 days"), and otherwise the heading says "since 22:20", "since yesterday 22:20" or "since Mon 22:20", with the tooltip "Last opened on this device" (a capped window says "Last opened on this device more than 7 days ago", since it no longer starts at the visit).

`sinceLines` builds lines from complete entity collections, strictly after the window starts, and never from `data.events`, which holds only the newest 100 events and records daemon delivery saves as the user. Settled runs, review-seat reservations excluded, become "Writer drafted Acme Robotics" (primary), "Scout's fit evaluation on Lumen Labs failed" (rose) or "Stopped: Writer's reply on Interview prep" (no tone). A run the daemon found unfinished when it started reads "...was interrupted when Pitchcrew stopped (found at restart, HH:MM)", has no tone and counts as interrupted, never failed; its only saved time is the restart that found it, so the line names that time for what it is and shows no clock in the time column, and it sorts by the restart. A deleted routine is "a routine", and a routine run that started more than five minutes late adds "(scheduled HH:MM, started HH:MM)". New items read "{kind label} · {subject}" with their Needs you bead: ochre, or rose for a failed request. New default instruction versions are left out: they carry only a release date, ranked from midnight UTC, so no clock time says when one arrived (`datedAsk` in `attention.ts`, which also keeps that date out of a row's age and a fold's "oldest"). Applied tracking signals read "Recorded Screening for Bluefin", moss for positive outcomes, and never name Tracker, because applied signals store no actor. New leads make one "N new leads" line that opens Pipeline itself: it counts every new lead, scored or not, so it never leads to a narrower view such as Not scored. A line about a job with an item opens that item, else the job, else the run, and says where it leads ("In Needs you", "Job details", "Conversation" or "Pipeline"). Lines are newest first and grouped by day with `dayLabel` ("Today", "Yesterday", a date); at most 40 show, then "{n} more in Activity". Never write "overnight" or "while you were away": Pitchcrew records crew work only while it is running, and nothing here claims what happened while it was stopped. An empty section says "Nothing recorded since {time}." with that explainer. The summary ("3 finished · 1 failed · 2 new asks", zero parts left out; its new asks are those that arrived in the window and still wait, so one drops out once decided) labels the button beside the Needs you heading, "Since you were last here: {summary} ↓" ("nothing recorded" when empty); it scrolls the work area, never the document, to the section and focuses its heading. On desks under 600px it reads "Since {time} ↓". ## Layout, setup and phones

Home is a standard page with the shared title row and no `WorkspaceHeaderPrimary`. It scrolls inside `.workspace-body` (`.home-workspace-body`), never the document, which the desktop smoke checks; the body is positioned, so screen-reader-only text inside it cannot stretch the document. The title fact reads "{n} need you · {active} of {concurrency} slots · {k} failed" ("1 needs you"; the failed part is rose and shown only when k > 0, the slots part only with queue data); at zero it starts "Nothing needs you" ("Nothing needs you · 0 of 5 slots" with queue data). DOM order is reading order: setup band, work strip, Needs you, Since, rail. From 880px of work width (container queries on the body) the desk, up to 784px, sits beside a sticky rail, its first heading level with the desk's (also when a rail under 300px puts the slot fact under its heading), that scrolls on its own. The rail's 12px top and bottom borders hold no content and take a `--shade-soft` shade (with a `--border` hairline in dark) through `data-more-above` and `data-more-below`, never a mask over text, and both its rests land on whole rows: `useRailEnd` sizes the rail so its first view, scrolled to the top, ends between rows (`--rail-room`, giving up only the row the view would cut and the gap above it, never more than 160px, so the foot sits a little above the view's bottom) and never under a block heading without its first row ("Pipeline 114 open" alone), since `restEnd` counts a heading and the line after it as one, and pads its end so its last view starts on one (`--rail-end`); its rows snap so other rests start on a whole row too. The work strip is hidden. While setup is open the checklist takes the desk's column above Needs you, with the rail beside it from the top. Between 560 and 879px the strip, desk and Since form one column with the rail's blocks below in two columns, and below 560px everything is one column. On phones (below 48rem) the rail's blocks and Since become closed sections through `ResponsiveDisclosure`, one DOM, so nothing remounts and focus survives a resize; this rule wins over the width rules. The sections follow the DOM: Since you were last here, Crew at work (Working now, Up next and the crew line) and Pipeline, each trigger showing its title over a short fact. Inside a section its own block heading and Working now's slot fact are for screen readers only, since the trigger, the title fact and the strip already say them. The work strip ("Working now", "{active} of {concurrency} slots" and a line per run; absent while nothing runs) is a label column beside its runs from 712px of work width. Each run's step is in its tooltip; on phones, which show no tooltip, it takes a second line under the job, clamped to two lines as the rail's rows clamp theirs. Narrower, its heading takes its own line and pins while the runs scroll away, casting `--shade` once stuck, with a `--border` hairline in dark.

Needs you is an h2 with an ochre count chip, hidden at zero, over one recessed tray holding a single-value `Accordion`. Each tier is a group (a `fieldset`, whose implicit role is `group`; Oxlint prefers the element to `role="group"`) labelled by its sticky caption; captions are not headings, because the shared `AccordionTrigger` already wraps each row in an h3. The outline is h1 Home; h2 Needs you, Since you were last here and the rail's blocks (Working now, Up next, Crew, which is for screen readers only, and Pipeline), plus the work strip's own "Working now" where the strip shows (below 880px of work width both are visible, except on phones, where the rail's sits in its closed section); h3 the rows and Since's day labels. The captions are 1 "Ends soon or stops the crew", 2 "Queued work waits on you", 3 "Your move on a job", 4 "A record to confirm", 5 "Changes nothing until you adopt it". A tier that still holds a live row keeps its caption; each run of adjacent tiers that hold only records shares one "Decided" caption and one group (`deskSections` in `packages/ui/src/HomePage/helpers.ts`), its records in rank order, so a finished morning reads as one list of what was decided and screen readers hear one group, never the same caption once per tier. A closed row shows a bead (ochre, rose for a failed request), the subject over its kind label ("Question from Writer"), the ask in at most two lines, its age ("4 min", Home's long time style; omitted when unknown or when it is only a release date, and read to screen readers as "asked just now" rather than "waiting" under a minute) and a chevron. Below 600px of desk the subject and kind share the first line while both fit whole, else the kind takes a line of its own, so it is never cut; a subject longer than its room ends in an ellipsis at every width. Rows are at least 48px, with a 44px pitch on phones. The open row is the tray's one raised surface and the only place on the page for a glazed action. In each tier, rows from the fourth on fold behind "{n} more · {kinds} · oldest {age}" ("{n} decided" once all are records), except tier 1 and rose items, which never fold; opening a folded row opens its fold first. The top item is open on load.

While onboarding is in setup, the setup checklist (`HomeSetup`) comes first, capped to the work area with its own scroll so Set up later stays reachable at 568×320 and 320×480. Its Finish setup is an outline button, because the glazed action belongs to the open row. Getting started in the sidebar opens Home; Pipeline with no jobs shows only its empty state. With no items the desk ends with "Nothing needs you right now." and what the crew is doing: the jobs it is working on, else "The crew is working." while only job-free runs execute, else that it is idle until the next enabled routine, else "The crew is idle."; with only records left, "Nothing else needs you. The crew carries on."; with no jobs outside setup, an outline Add your first job and Open Pipeline.

Phones keep 44px targets and never scroll sideways at 390px: standalone navigation is a `Button` rendering a `Link`, and row-shaped links get `min-height: 44px` below 48rem or with a coarse pointer, while inline prose links keep their line height. Proposal cards take their materials from `packages/ui/src/styles/proposals.css` and are flattened inside a row. The open body declares `container: decide`, so decision footers reflow as they do in Decisions. Never style descendants through an ancestor `:has()`; state comes from rendered attributes such as `data-open`, `data-tone`, `data-kind`, `data-more-above` and `data-more-below`. Record lines enter with `clay-settle-in`, which reduced motion turns into a fade, and from 600px of desk sit on the rows' grid, the subject in its column beside the outcome; narrower, the outcome sits under the subject. Row panels, folds, Up next's fold and the phone sections clip to their own moving edge while they open (`overflow: clip` with a 6px `overflow-clip-margin`, enough for focus rings and hover bands), so an opening row never draws over the rows below; never un-clip them by state, since Base UI drops `data-starting-style` a frame into the 200 ms morph.
