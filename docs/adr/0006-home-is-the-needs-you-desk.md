# Make Home the Needs you desk and move the board to Pipeline

Design decision accepted on 2026-11-19. A design debate voted 8 to 1 that the Kanban board is a good map of applications but a poor control surface for the crew: its Needs you deck covered four attention kinds and decided nothing in place, tracking reviews sat in a separate strip above it, and failed requests, crew proposals and Profile updates lived on other pages. `/` is now Home: one ranked Needs you list of every attention item with the top item open, each item decided in place through the existing gated components and endpoints where those components can decide it, else opened in its existing form or page, beside the crew's live work (Working now, Up next, a crew line and pipeline stage counts) and what was recorded since the user was last here. Decisions made on Home leave a one-line record for the session; items resolved elsewhere simply leave. The board moved to Pipeline at `/pipeline`, which has a Table mode with saved views in the URL, keys scoped to the table and a bulk Evaluate fit that queues one board action per job and reports each job's outcome. Crew opens with an At work lens: one row per busy agent listing its job-owned runs, waits and queued, held or failed requests, with idle agents folded. The sidebar's primary places are Home, Pipeline, Crew and Profile (⌘1–4); Decisions moved under More and keeps `/inbox`, its history and deep links.

We considered keeping the board at `/` with bands above the columns, which pushed the pipeline below the fold; a time-ordered Today page; an agent-first control room as home, which made agents the subject and risked implying that each agent serializes its own work; and the job ledger as home, which put attention behind a filter. The lens and the ledger keep their strengths as views on Crew and Pipeline without a second home.

Nothing new approves an outward action: Home reuses the Decisions, browser approval, question, tracking, queue recovery, proposal and writing-guide controls with their server checks, and links out where a decision needs a full form. Neither the table nor the lens decides an item: an ask in either opens it on Home, running work opens its run, and the lens acts on work only through a running job or conversation queue's own Pause, Resume and Stop menu; bulk Evaluate fit queues board actions, which are not outward actions, through the existing endpoint. One helper derives every attention count, so the Home badge, its title fact and its list agree; Crew and Profile keep their own badges over the same items. Scheduling stays per job ([schedule work by job](0002-schedule-work-by-job.md)): the rail, the table's Queue column and the lens are views over job-owned work and share one waiting line. ⌘2 now opens Pipeline instead of Decisions. Analytics page names changed from `board` to `home` and `pipeline`. "Since you were last here" relies on a device-local checkpoint and entity timestamps, so it reports only what was recorded and never claims Pitchcrew was running meanwhile. See [Home](../home.md) and [Pipeline](../board.md).
